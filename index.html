What is the Grade Calculator?

The Grade Calculator combines your scores across assignments, tests, and exams — each with its own weight — into a single overall course grade. It can also tell you the score you still need on a remaining assessment to reach a target.

How it works

Your overall grade is the weighted average of each component: Grade = Σ(score × weight) ÷ Σ(weight). To find the mark you need on a final worth weight w, rearrange to needed = (target − current × (1 − w)) ÷ w, where current is your average on the work completed so far.

Example 1 — Weighted average. Homework 80% (weight 30%), midterm 75% (30%), final 90% (40%): 0.8×30 + 0.75×30 + 0.9×40 = 24 + 22.5 + 36 = 82.5%.

Example 2 — Score needed. You have 85% going into a final worth 40% and want an overall 80%: needed = (80 − 85×0.6) ÷ 0.4 = (80 − 51) ÷ 0.4 = 72.5% on the final.

When to use it

  • Checking your current standing partway through a course.
  • Working out the exam score needed to pass or hit a target.
  • Seeing how a low assignment affects your final grade.
  • Comparing how different weighting schemes change the result.
